      Each comment lands in its strongest cluster only, so nothing is counted twice. Comments that match none stay unassigned instead of being forced. Distinguishing words come from the corpus itself, ranked by log-odds against the rest.

      Deduction that wins the misread score

      Charge mix

      A comment counts as confused when it names a deduction and signals surprise: hidden, extra, undisclosed, bina bataye, pata nahi. Volume alone would crown the best-known fee, so the score multiplies how often a charge is named by how often it is misread.

      Model drafts

      Counts do not move: clusters, tallies and the fee finding stay computed. A language model reads the pack below and writes the parts a person would usually write: the observation, three actions, which three comments to quote, and the support snippet. Each draft is checked against that pack. A failed draft goes back once with the reasons; if it fails again, the rule-based version stays.
